## Scene 1: Turn Copilot's Secret Notebook Into Your Own App! (`00:00:00 – 00:00:26`)

Welcome to the Chronicle Fundamentals Workshop! Have you ever wondered where your GitHub Copilot chats go after you close Visual Studio Code? Every question you ask, every thinking bubble, every command, and every code change is saved inside a hidden detective notebook on your computer. Today, in just sixty fun minutes, you are going to build Chronicle—your very own local web app to explore it all!

## Scene 2: Zero Outside Packages — Own LEGO Bricks Only! (`00:00:26 – 00:00:55`)

Here is our coolest superpower today: the Zero-Package Rule! Most apps download hundreds of outside mystery boxes from the internet. If even one box breaks, the whole castle falls down! Instead, we will build every single piece using the clean LEGO bricks already built into Node.js and your web browser. Zero npm installs, zero node_modules, and lightning-fast startup!

## Scene 3: Quick Backpack Check & Open Your Empty Sandbox (`00:00:55 – 00:01:15`)

We kick off in Lab Zero with an easy seven-minute backpack check. Check that Node version twenty-two or newer is ready in your terminal, open the empty sampleapp folder directly in VS Code, and switch Copilot Chat into Agent mode with a frontier model like Claude Sonnet, GPT, or Gemini!

## Scene 4: Write Copilot's Magic Rulebook! (`00:01:15 – 00:01:36`)

Next, in Lab One, you paste your first prompt to create Copilot's magic rulebook: dot-github slash copilot-instructions-dot-md. From that moment on, VS Code automatically hands your rulebook to Copilot on every single prompt—so Copilot remembers never to install outside packages!

## Scene 5: Build the Chat Reader & Spot-the-Difference Engine (`00:01:36 – 00:01:59`)

In Lab Two, Copilot builds the brain of your app! It learns how to read both classic VS Code chats and new Copilot Agent sessions, indexes every word in a speedy background worker, and builds a Spot-the-Difference engine so deleted lines glow red and new lines glow green. Then you run node --test and watch green checkmarks light up your terminal!

## Scene 6: Lock the Front Door with Your Own node:http Server (`00:01:59 – 00:02:23`)

In Lab Three, you build a fast, private local web server in src/server.js using Node's built-in http module—no Express required! It locks the door to 127.0.0.1 so random websites can never peek at your chats, and automatically finds your computer's default VS Code and Agent session folders!

## Scene 7: Paint Dark & Bright White Mode, Sort & Read Full Stories! (`00:02:23 – 00:02:44`)

Finally, in Lab Four, two fun prompts paint your entire browser playground! You will create over sixty crisp SVG icons, a Markdown highlighter and HTML safety shield, easy sorting by Date or Name, and a one-click toggle between sleek Dark Mode and super-clear Bright White Mode!

## Scene 8: Launch Chronicle in your browser (`00:02:44 – 00:03:02`)

At the finish line, use the Node start command in Lab Four, open the local address it prints, and even find the very workshop conversation you are having right now! Open Lab Zero in VS Code, trust your empty sampleapp folder, and let's build Chronicle together!
